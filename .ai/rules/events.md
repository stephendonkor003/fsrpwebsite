# Events

## Group event media in dated day folders
Store event gallery files under public/media/events/{event-slug}/{YYYY-MM-DD}-day-{NN}/photos or videos with normalized, zero-padded sequence filenames. App\EventGallery discovers JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, and WEBM files; copy source files without modifying them and verify gallery ordering plus public asset responses after each batch.
